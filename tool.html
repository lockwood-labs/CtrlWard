How blindability works

Expected win rate

Your average matchup win rate, weighted by how often each enemy is actually picked. A 60% matchup into a champion nobody plays barely moves it; a 46% matchup into the most popular pick in the role drags it down every single queue.

Spread

The weighted standard deviation of those matchup win rates — the width of the bell curve. A narrow spread means the game plays out roughly the same no matter who shows up. A wide spread means some lobbies are free and some are doomed before lane starts.

Counter risk

The share of enemy picks — again weighted by pick rate — where your win rate drops below 47%. This is the “how likely is a counterpick, actually?” number: a nightmare matchup that appears in 1% of games is a footnote, not a risk.

The score

Expected win rate, penalized for spread and counter risk. That's why a flat 51% champion outranks a coin-flip 51% champion here — blind picking is about the floor, not the ceiling.

score = 50 + (expWR − 50) × 7 − max(0, spread − 3.5) × 5 − counterRisk × 0.40

Use your own matchup numbers

The built-in dataset is a model: every champion's matchup curve is generated from their archetype, a per-champion strength and volatility rating, and curated hard counters — shaped to behave like the real thing, but not scraped live stats. If you track real numbers (from Lolalytics, U.GG, OP.GG or your own games), paste them as CSV for the currently selected role and everything recomputes instantly, for this session:

champion,enemy,winrate — one row per matchup, e.g. Rammus,Master Yi,61.2. Names can be champion IDs (MasterYi) or display names (Master Yi). Rows you import replace the modeled matchup in both directions; matchups you don't list keep the model values.
